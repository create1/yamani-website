Built for the World, Not Just One City

Apotheos is online-first. Attend any class live from anywhere — geography is never a barrier to access.

Every Apotheos class streams live online simultaneously with our in-person sessions. Whether you're across town or across the world, you get the same instructor, the same real-time chat, and the same transformative experience.

The Online Experience:

  • Live classes with real instructors — every session streams live with two-way interaction, not pre-recorded video
  • Shared community chat — one conversation connecting remote and in-room students in real time
  • Session recordings & materials — watch anything you missed on-demand, slides and resources included
  • Online membership from $49/mo — full access to the digital platform from anywhere in the world

Also available in-person. Our physical campus is at Stone House — 107 Sacramento Street, Nevada City, CA — a beautifully restored historic building with dedicated learning spaces, coworking, podcast studios, and wellness facilities for local members.
